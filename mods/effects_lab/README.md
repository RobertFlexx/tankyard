# Effects Lab

Needs Tank Yard 1.1.0 or later. This pack adds the **Effects Lab** map without changing tank stats or game rules.

Enable it on **Mods**, then choose Effects Lab in an offline Sandbox game. In the Arena Workshop, **Files → Import pack** opens its map for editing.

- The lantern floats and sways from the start of the round.
- Enter **GROW + CHIME** to enlarge the arrow. Its arrival sounds a chime and flashes the separate neon. The trip back counts as another arrival.
- Enter **STOP LANTERN** to freeze its pose. Restart the preview or round to start it again.
- Drive onto **LAUNCH** to be flung diagonally into open floor.
- The grow area adds a short camera shake. Reduced motion disables shake and animation; tints and sizes remain visible.

Follow [Animate and effects](../../docs/effects-tutorial.md) to build it yourself. The map uses ordinary JSON triggers; it has no script or editor plugin.
