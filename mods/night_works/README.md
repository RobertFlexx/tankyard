# Night Works

Requires Tank Yard 1.3 (protocol 29).

A dark yard that shows what the 1.3 workshop can do with physics, paths and lighting. Every piece is plain map data, so open it in the workshop to see how it is built.

- **Push door** (group 2): a slab across the north room's doorway. Drive into it and it gives way into the room, then springs shut behind you.
- **Crate** (group 3): a free body that tanks push and shots knock about. Shove it onto the green **crate plate** and the **vault gate** (group 4) opens with a chime. Push it off and the gate closes again.
- **Ferry** (group 5): a platform on a bouncing path, with a beam riding along on top.
- **Shockwave ring** (group 6): every six seconds the middle of the yard blasts tanks outward, shakes the camera and pulses the ring.
- **Lights plate**: drive onto it and the yard brightens for ten seconds, then sinks back into the dark.
- **Nightfall**: two minutes in, the yard sinks to 80% darkness over six seconds.
- **Fling pad**, **ice**, a **gravity well** and a **repair pad** fill out the floor.
- The rooms have roofs that fade as you drive under them, and the vault's sign appears only when you are close.

Import the folder on Mods, enable it and choose Night Works in Offline modes, or start a server with `--mod mods/night_works --map night_works`. The workshop can import its `mod.json` to edit it.
