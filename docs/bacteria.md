# Bacteria

Bacteria is a level-55 escape tank with twin alternating cannons and a ten-second pocket dimension. Evolve from **Specter** or **Wisp**, or select Bacteria in Sandbox.

Press the ability key shown above your XP bar to noclip out of the arena. A short impact plays locally, the screen dips to black, and you arrive in a private maze of yellow wallpaper, worn carpet and fluorescent lights. Move normally through its rooms while your hull repairs. No other tanks, bosses or bullets enter your pocket.

## The visit

- The pocket lasts **10 seconds** and repairs **40% of maximum health**, spread across the visit and capped at full health.
- You can move and aim, but cannot fire, deal damage, take damage, collect a core or contest an objective. Existing shots and deployed weapons are cleared when you enter, and a carried core drops at your entry point.
- The maze has its own walls. Returning puts you at the corresponding arena position, clear of cover and within the arena border.
- A countdown warns before the return. Opponents see a small return ring during the last 0.8 seconds.
- The ability recharges for **40 seconds from entry**, leaving **30 seconds outside** between full visits. Hold your escape for when you need it: the cannons trade some firepower for that recovery window.

The ambience and entry sound use **Master volume** and **Effects volume**. They play only on your client. Reduced motion keeps the lights steady and uses a slower entry fade. `--no-audio` silences the dimension too.
