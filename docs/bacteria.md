# Bacteria

Bacteria is a level-55 escape tank with twin alternating cannons and a pocket dimension that lasts up to ten seconds. Evolve from **Specter** or **Wisp**, or select Bacteria in Sandbox.

Press the ability key shown above your XP bar to noclip out of the arena. A short impact plays locally, the screen dips to black, and you arrive in a private maze of yellow wallpaper, worn carpet and fluorescent lights. Move normally through its rooms while your hull repairs. Other players, bosses and arena bullets cannot enter your pocket, but its doubles can chase you out.

## The visit

- The pocket lasts up to **10 seconds** and repairs **4% of maximum health each second**, capped at full health. A full visit repairs 40%.
- You can move and aim, but cannot fire, deal damage, take damage, collect a core or contest an objective. Existing shots and deployed weapons are cleared when you enter, and a carried core drops at your entry point.
- Three silent doubles wait in nearby rooms, copying your tank and other living players' tank models. They stand still for about two seconds, then follow you through the doorways, a little slower than you can drive. They cannot shoot. Touching one, even while it is still, sends you back early without dealing damage. You keep the healing already earned.
- The rooms sit on a regular grid, with doorways in the middle or towards one end of each wall. Most fixtures hum steadily; a few flicker or have died. The layout stays fixed when the shared arena changes size. Returning puts you at the corresponding arena position, clear of cover and within the arena border.
- A countdown warns before the return. Opponents see a small return ring during the last 0.8 seconds.
- The ability recharges for **40 seconds from entry**, leaving **30 seconds outside** between full visits. A catch does not reset or shorten the cooldown, so an early return leaves you exposed for longer.

The softened, translucent ceiling lights sit above tanks and shift gently against the floor as you move. The ambience and entry sound use **Master volume** and **Effects volume** and play only on your client. Reduced motion keeps the lights steady, holds failing tubes at a low glow, disables the parallax and uses a slower entry fade. `--no-audio` silences the dimension too.

The wallpaper and carpet are from Amini Allight's CC0 [Backrooms Textures](https://github.com/amini-allight/backrooms-textures). The light hum is a CC0 recording by [theonlycanvas](https://freesound.org/people/theonlycanvas/sounds/234462/), mixed with original low room tones and duct noise. See [third-party notices](../NOTICE.md) for credits.

The doubles need **1.1.0 or later (protocol 28)** on players, servers and any separate relay. Bacteria first appeared in 1.0.1, which has the pocket without doubles.
