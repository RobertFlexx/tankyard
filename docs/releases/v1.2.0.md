# Tank Yard v1.2.0

Type exact values in the workshop, use team symbols with your preferred colors, and adjust effects volume while sounds are playing. This release also fixes trigger arrivals, creator-boss abilities, connection stalls and unsafe asset loading. Everything in [v1.1.0](v1.1.0.md) is included.

## Workshop and controls

- Click a slider's value to type a number, calculation such as `4000/2`, or a relative change such as `+=50`. Enter applies, Escape cancels, and Tab or Shift+Tab moves between fields. Invalid values stay in the field without changing the map or setting.
- Hold Shift while dragging a slider for finer control. Fractional dimensions and plugin Number values keep their precision even when the slider has a coarse drag step; counts and color channels remain whole numbers.
- The inspector scrolls focused numbers into view and supports a draggable scrollbar, Page Up/Down, Home and End. Color controls accept `#RGB` and `#RRGGBB`.
- Multi-selection adds **Space X/Y evenly** and **Match widest/tallest**. Each arrangement is one undo step, and separately submitted numbers have separate undo steps.
- Numeric input takes priority over editor and gameplay shortcuts. Clicking away applies a valid edit; an invalid edit stays focused.
- Render scale keeps exact typed percentages, such as 123%, after saving and restarting.

See the [workshop guide](../map-editor.md) and [editor plugin guide](../editor-plugins.md) for controls and examples.

## Accessibility and audio

- Choose **Red/green**, **Blue/yellow** or **Separated** palettes with a live preview. Team symbols can also stay enabled with the original colors, including on captured objectives and the minimap.
- Accessibility reset restores those options, interface scale, high contrast and reduced motion without changing other settings.
- Tank previews and their textures stay aligned when enlarged menus scroll.
- **Effects volume** controls map sounds, boss alarms and pocket ambience. Master and Effects volume now adjust map sounds already playing, including workshop previews.
- Sound samples are prepared in the background and cached individually. Repeated cues coalesce, overlapping playback is bounded, and alarm pitch changes keep a continuous waveform.
- Audio-device failures use bounded retries and distinguish microphone errors from speaker errors. A microphone failure no longer stops healthy playback.

## Gameplay and stability

- Motions with very short return delays correctly report both their outward and return arrivals to **On arrive** triggers.
- Axiom clears transient class effects when changing form. Its underlying tank ability cannot activate during creator-boss form, in either server simulation or prediction.
- Fully obstructed creator-boss attacks leave their cooldown available. Repulse can clear nearby hostile shots at the projectile limit, and Reforge explains when the hull is already full.
- Boss drones reuse squared target distances instead of repeatedly calculating square roots while choosing a target.
- Non-ASCII community passwords no longer crash when shortened to the byte limit. Settings use private file permissions on Unix and replace the previous profile only after a complete write.

## Security and connections

- SVG mod assets cannot load images from files outside the pack, including through nested SVG data URLs. Keep SVG images self-contained; external file references are ignored.
- Pack and project readers reject special files that could stall loading. Directory packs enforce their entry limit while scanning, including ignored metadata files.
- Client WebSocket and TLS upgrades have an absolute handshake deadline, preventing slow replies from keeping connection workers occupied indefinitely. Established connections continue normally after that deadline.
- Status probes process incoming datagrams in bounded batches, so a flood of invalid replies cannot prevent a probe from finishing.

## Compatibility

Protocol **28** is unchanged. Tank Yard 1.1.0 and 1.2.0 clients and servers play together; updating both sides gives you the simulation fixes as well as the client improvements. Existing maps, packs and settings remain supported. Clients from 1.0.1 and earlier still need to update.
