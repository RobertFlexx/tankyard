# Tank Yard v2.1.8

Bosses and tank bots now investigate the last spot where they saw an opponent who slipped behind cover. They keep moving even when the detour takes a while, then pick up the chase if the opponent comes back into view.

Autonomous drones, guided rounds and pocket-dimension doubles also use their last sighting when a wall cuts off their view. Protocol **35** still supports standard matches with earlier 2.1.x clients.
