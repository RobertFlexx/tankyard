Progression takes longer, bots fight at closer range, and Trapper sets up faster.

- XP thresholds rise gradually: level 15 needs 1,020 XP, level 30 needs 8,644, and level 45 needs 35,304. Tank upgrades still unlock at 15 / 30 / 45.
- Skills are spread across the match: three points per four levels, ending at the same 33-point build. Each skill still caps at seven.
- Bot sight is shorter at every difficulty. Automatic mounts respect the same limit. Bots withdraw from nearby drones and approaching homing rounds, with walls blocking their detection.
- Small neural models learn tank traits and skill allocations from each bot's own results. Their preferences supplement normal combat decisions, with bounded memory and training. Reaction delays, aim error, and player stat limits still apply. Learning lasts across respawns until the match ends.
- Riposte has a 0.5-second parry window, a 4.2-second ability cooldown, and a slightly faster 0.62-second cannon reload. Returned shots keep the existing homing limits.
- Bots learn from visible human movement and class results. They remember who hurt or killed them, gain fear or animosity, and respond differently according to temperament. Winning and time ease grudges; memory resets each match. Hidden players stay hidden.
- Trapper reload drops from 1.5 to 1.25 seconds. Trap damage rises from 20 to 23 and penetration from 4 to 4.5. Lifetime and the 24-trap limit stay the same. Barricader, Auto Trapper, Mega Trapper, Triple Trapper, Overtrapper, and Gunner Trapper also receive small trap buffs.
- Automatic mounts stop wasting shots beyond their flight range. Auto Smasher bullets expire after 2.2 seconds, and its bots use body damage instead of circling with the small gun.
