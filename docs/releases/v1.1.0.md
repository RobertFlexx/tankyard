# Tank Yard v1.1.0

The Arena Workshop gains effects: decor that floats, sways, pulses, changes color and grows, launch pads, sounds and screen shake. Bacteria's pocket dimension becomes a maze of rooms with doubles that hunt you down. Scripts and JSON packs can use every new piece. Everything in [v1.0.1](v1.0.1.md) is included.

## Workshop effects

- **Animate decor** plays a looping animation on a group's decor: **Bob**, **Sway**, **Throb**, **Shake** or **Orbit**, each with its own size and pace. The five play together, so one group can bob and sway at once, and changing the pace never makes it jump.
- **Tint decor** mixes a color into a group's decor, lights and particles. A short tint that goes back makes a flash.
- **Scale decor** draws a group's decor and emitters from 0 to 400% of their placed size, particles included.
- **Stop group** halts a group's moves, turns, fades, tints, sizes and animations where they are and cancels its trip back.
- **Shake screen** shakes the views of the tanks inside a trigger's area. Players' **Camera shake** setting scales it, and Reduced motion turns it off.
- **Play sound** plays one of eight sounds at a trigger: Chime, Bell, Alarm, Switch, Whoosh, Rumble, Fanfare and Thump. Players hear it by distance, at their Effects volume.
- Tint, Scale and Animate play like motions, with a length, an easing curve and **Go back after**. **On arrive** watches them too.
- **Launch pads** fling tanks along their direction, then let them coast for a moment before steering takes over. Prediction uses the server's own code, so launches feel immediate online.
- A new **Effects** tab in the palette holds thirteen ready-made pieces: Float, Sway, Throb, Orbit, Quiver, Flash, Alarm, Dye, Grow, Stop, Quake, Chime and Siren.
- The logic preview plays effects: the canvas shakes when the test tank is caught in a Shake screen, and Play sound triggers play through your speakers.
- Effects change only how decor looks. Blocks, pads and trigger areas keep their shape and place, and Reduced motion holds animations still while keeping colors and sizes.

## Bacteria

- The pocket dimension is now a grid of rooms with aligned walls. Doorways use several positions along the room grid, and the walls continue past the edge of the maze.
- Soft, translucent ceiling lights hang above your tank with a gentle parallax. A few fixtures flicker and some have died.
- Up to three doubles of your tank wake after a short pause and follow you through the rooms. If one catches you, you're forced out early. They don't shoot, and only the server moves them.
- Bacteria's model has new shoulder plates that leave your team color visible.

## Modding

- New script functions: `tint_map_group`, `scale_map_group`, `animate_map_group`, `stop_map_group`, `shake_view` and `play_sound`, and `map_pose` to read where a group's motions have put it.
- JSON hooks gain `map_tint`, `map_animate`, `map_stop`, `shake_view` and `play_sound`, and `map_motion` accepts `scale`.
- JSON maps gain the `launch` pad and the `tint`, `scale`, `animate`, `stop`, `shake` and `sound` trigger actions. New fields are left out of exported maps when unused.
- Workshop plugins see the new trigger fields, and `editor.free_group` skips groups that effects use.
- A new example pack, [`mods/arena_fx`](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/tree/v1.1.0/mods/arena_fx), rings a bell at the start of each round, marks wrecks and kill streaks, and gives maps an alarm they can sound by name.

## Sandbox and hosting

- Sandbox has a centered toolbox with tank previews, search by name or ID, tier filters, touchpad paging and separate player and spawn controls. Escape closes it, and search input takes priority over gameplay shortcuts.
- Dedicated hosts can create a token-authenticated config with `--secure --write-config`, check it with `--check-config`, and issue short-lived UDP invitations with `--issue-token`. Keys are never printed, and existing credential files are never replaced.
- Invalid private config errors no longer quote credential lines.
- The multiplayer load tool supports runs up to 24 hours, scheduled reconnects and JSON reports, with bounded timing storage.
- Local crash reports survive the next launch. `tankyard --diagnostics-path` prints their folder; reports are never uploaded automatically.
- Menu pages and bot decision subsystems are split into smaller modules. Additional sandbox checks cover hostile effect arguments, module cycles, path escapes and isolation between packs.

## Compatibility

Protocol **28** carries the doubles and the new effects. Update clients, servers and any separate relays together; 1.0.1 and earlier cannot join a 1.1.0 server, and older peers receive an update notice. Maps from earlier versions open unchanged. Maps that use launch pads or effects need 1.1.0 to open and play.
