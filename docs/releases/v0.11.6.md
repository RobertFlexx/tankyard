# Tank Yard v0.11.6

Know who got you, and who took down the boss. Everything in [v0.11.5](v0.11.5.md) is included.

## Kills

- When another tank destroys you, a card shows who did it: their tank, name, level and class, and their score counting up. It then fades and the camera moves over to spectate them. Left / Right or **Previous** / **Next** still switch who you watch. With reduced motion on, the card simply fades in and out.
- Spectating now really starts on your killer. It used to pick the top scorer, because the game forgot your attacker as soon as you died. In team modes you still watch a teammate.
- When a boss falls, the banner at the top reads "**Fallen Overlord was killed by** …", naming the player who did the most damage to it. Players on older versions see it as a regular kill notice. Mod kill hooks still fire only for tank kills.

## Mod file browser

- Hold the left mouse button and drag to highlight text in the code view, then press Cmd+C (Ctrl+C on Windows and Linux) to copy it. Cmd/Ctrl+A selects the whole file.
- Code blocks in rendered Markdown have a copy button, and their text can be highlighted too.
- List items that continue onto an indented next line now render as one item.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x and v0.11.x clients and servers play together.
