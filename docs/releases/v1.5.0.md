# Tank Yard v1.5.0

Build signs, waypoints, player markers and larger menus directly in the workshop. Moving crates cost less, cover stays in step with tanks, and beams cast softer light. Everything in [v1.4.0](v1.4.0.md) is included.

## Interfaces in the arena

- **Floating signs and tank markers.** A Show UI trigger can place its widget over its area or above the tank that set it off. Area markers follow their group's doors, platforms, paths and pushed bodies. Hidden groups and tanks take their markers out of view.
- **Direction arrows.** Turn on *Point the way when out of view* to keep a marker in an inset area, clear of the normal HUD, with an arrow toward its target.
- **Six choices**, arranged in a row, a column or a two-column grid. Oversized menus shrink to fit small windows and workshop previews. A choice takes priority over controls underneath it.
- **32 map UI slots.** Scores, timers, signs and menus can each have their own slot. Scripts retain their separate widget budgets; each player receives at most 64 widgets in total.
- **Glow and seven new icons:** crown, skull, lock, check, cross, fire and bomb. These use the same geometric style as the existing interface. Reduced motion still removes movement from entrances and pulses.
- **`{cause}` text** names the tank that set off an announcement or UI trigger, including through signals and delays. `{name}` remains the viewer's name. A trigger without a tank leaves `{cause}` empty.
- **Workshop presets** include Sign and Tank marker, with the same live designer preview and clickable logic preview used for screen widgets.
- **Arcade Hall** now has a six-choice shop, a vault waypoint, a help card, stage lighting and a marker over the vault winner. Its map data is included in every download.

Scripts can use `pin`, `point`, `layout` and `glow` in `tankyard.hud`. A coordinate pin can also name a `group` to follow its motion. See [modding](../modding.md#hud-widgets) and the [workshop UI guide](../map-editor.md#ui).

The [menus and waypoints tutorial](../ui-tutorial.md) includes a [short video](../ui-tutorial.mp4) recorded in the workshop and a playtest.

## Physics and lighting

- Bot navigation updates the cells near moving cover instead of rebuilding the whole route grid whenever a crate slides. Nearby-wall queries also avoid repeated scans across a large maze.
- Offline moving cover is interpolated at the same time as tanks, smoothing pushed crates and gliding doors between simulation ticks.
- Beams have soft ends, a wider pool of light and a fading travelling highlight. Nearby walls, tanks, shapes and bosses catch light along their edges.
- Arrowheads, chevrons, rings and pad marks meet without overlapping fills, removing darker seams in translucent decorations.

## Fixes

- Separate labels retain their own animations even when placed on top of one another. Moving or updating a widget does not leave fading copies behind.
- Menus keep their shape while fading out. Starting another match clears the previous match's widget animations.
- Menus sliding into place capture clicks along their entrance path, and screen menus do not also activate HUD controls underneath.
- Small designer previews and oversized widgets cannot invert the layout's clamp range.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.5.0 uses **protocol 31**. Update clients, servers and separate relays together; 1.4.0 uses protocol 30. Earlier maps and packs still open. Maps using the new pins, layouts, icons or slots above 16 require 1.5.0.
