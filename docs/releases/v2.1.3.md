# Tank Yard v2.1.3

A small visual patch. Everything in [v2.1.2](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.3/docs/releases/v2.1.2.md) is included.

## Fix

- The Bastion mark in **Credits** now sits beside the two credit lines instead of floating above them. Its full height also counts toward the page's scroll range, so it cannot be clipped in a short window.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer still uses **protocol 35**. Tank Yard 2.1.3, 2.1.2, 2.1.1 and 2.1.0 clients and servers can play together; hosts and players on 2.0 builds still need to update together.

See [hosting](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.3/docs/hosting.md), [bosses](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.3/docs/bosses-and-learning.md) and [modding](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.3/docs/modding.md).
