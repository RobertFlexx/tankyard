# Tank Yard v0.10.3

Much faster rendering, especially on Macs, and bots that play like people.

## Performance

- **MSAA no longer stalls the GPU.** Anti-aliased render targets were resolved after every draw call; they now resolve once per frame. With 30 bots on an Apple M5 at 2560×1664, the Performance preset with MSAA went from 88 ms to under 10 ms per frame, and Ultra from 84 ms to under 9 ms. Lower-power GPUs gain the most.
- **Macs with Apple silicon** cap MSAA at 4x. Their GPUs resolve 4 samples in tile memory for almost nothing, but emulate 8x at more than twice the cost with no visible difference. A saved 8x setting uses 4x automatically.
- Larger draw batches mean far fewer GPU passes per frame on every platform.
- FSR at 100% render scale no longer adds a sharpening pass; it draws the arena directly.
- Menus run at most 60 fps when vsync is off, so idle screens keep the fans quiet.

## Bots

- **Personalities**: every bot has its own aggression, patience, curiosity and sociability, which shape whom it fights, how close it plays, when it backs off, where it roams and whom it trusts. Stats, sight and reaction limits are the same for every bot.
- **No more standing around**: hurt bots heal while farming and roaming once the threat is gone, instead of freezing until they recover. Idle time fell from about 10% to 3%.
- **Circle-strafing** around targets instead of parking at range, with each bot reversing on its own rhythm.
- **Getting unstuck**: a bot that pushes without moving sidesteps, replans its route and, if that fails, rethinks its goal.
- **Purposeful roaming**: bots return to where they last saw action; team bots farm and push toward the front line; high-level tanks hunt players rather than shapes.
- **Relationships**: in free-for-all, players who share space peacefully slowly earn a wary truce, which one hit breaks. Grudges, fear and friendship from fights carry on as before.
- **Judging their tanks**: each bot keeps its own verdict on every class it plays. A branch that keeps getting it killed is avoided next life, and a class it doubts is played more carefully. Verdicts carry into the next round.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x clients and servers play together. Bots run on the server, so a server needs v0.10.3 for the new behaviour.
