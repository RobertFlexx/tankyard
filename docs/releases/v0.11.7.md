# Tank Yard v0.11.7

Lobbies of twenty, and bots that keep up. Everything in [v0.11.6](v0.11.6.md) is included.

## Lobbies

- Servers now hold up to 20 players per lobby by default (`arena_capacity = 20`), as do the shipped configs and **Host server**. Set `arena_capacity = 0` for one lobby of any size.
- When every lobby is full, or you pick one that just filled up, joining starts a new lobby instead of squeezing you into a full one.
- Lobbies no longer show as "1/1 Full" on servers without a lobby size. Those servers now report their real player limit, and older ones show how many are playing.
- The lobby list on the title screen shows three lobbies per page, with page arrows underneath once there are more. The mouse wheel flips pages too.

## Bots

- When one player's tank far outclasses the bots, they gradually play sharper, up to Expert, and ease back as the gap closes. The change is spread over tiny steps, so there's no sudden jump. Practice challenges keep their bots fixed.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x and v0.11.x clients and servers play together.
