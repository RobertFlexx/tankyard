# Tank Yard v0.15.0

The Arena Workshop gets a rebuilt editor, map logic you can test in place, and two new pads: conveyors and portals.

## Arena Workshop

- Switch between Build, Edit and Delete with 1, 2 and 3. Click to place a cell or drag to draw any size. Turn on Swipe to paint as you drag, or right-click an object to build copies of it.
- Drag across the floor to select a region, double-click to select a whole group, and Alt-drag to leave a copy behind. Turn, flip, align, resize and reorder from the dock or the keyboard, and copy one object's settings onto others. Undo keeps 48 steps.
- Click any value to type an exact number, or edit several objects at once. An object's group can start hidden right from its own panel, and the map id follows the arena's name.
- The Check tab lists anything that would stop a playtest or export and selects each problem on the canvas. Shift+F5 starts a playtest at the pointer, and F1 lists every shortcut.

## Map logic

- Triggers can now fire when a tank leaves an area, on a signal from another trigger, or when one of eight counters reaches a value. New actions send signals, add to counters and drop shapes.
- Limit a trigger to players, bots or one team, and give it a chance and a delay. Hiding a trigger's own group switches it off. Labels and announcements show counters with `{1}` to `{8}`.
- Press P to run the logic inside the editor and hold T to drive a test tank through it. L draws a line from each trigger to what it changes. The Logic palette has presets for plates, timers, signals, counters, goals, shape drops and boss calls.

## Pads

- Conveyors carry tanks along their arrows, and their marks move with the belt. Portals send tanks to an exit marker you drag into place.
- Conveyor and speed effects are predicted on your side, so movement stays smooth online.

## Fixes

- A drag along one grid line builds a block one cell deep instead of an invisible sliver. Grid lines draw evenly at every zoom.
- The Escape that ends a playtest no longer also clears the editor selection. The shortcut list no longer cuts off its descriptions.
- Save and export messages show short workshop paths, and the settings panel scrolls faster.
