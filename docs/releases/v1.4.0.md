# Tank Yard v1.4.0

Design your own on-screen interface. Map triggers and scripts can now show styled widgets (messages, counters, timers, lives and menus of choices) and react to what each player picks. Pushing crates online is smooth, and dark arenas draw faster. Everything in [v1.3.0](v1.3.0.md) is included.

## UI in the workshop

- **Show UI** puts a widget on players' screens: a title, a line of text with an icon, a bar and up to four choices, each part optional. **Hide UI** takes it away again, slot by slot or all at once.
- **Five looks:** plain text with a soft shadow, a panel, a card with a colored stripe, a banner across the screen and a pill. Pick one of 13 icons, the text size, the panel's opacity, and colors for the text, panel and accent, or leave them to follow each player's light or dark theme.
- **Place it anywhere.** Top, Middle and Bottom, Left, Center and Right jump to the usual spots, and *Across* and *Down* fine-tune it. Widgets stay inside the screen at any window size and interface scale.
- **Motion.** Widgets fade, pop, slide in from the nearer side or drop from above, fade out when they go and keep their place while their text changes. **Pulse** draws the eye. With **Reduced motion** on, they appear and go without movement.
- **Live text.** `{1}`–`{8}` show counters, `{left}` the seconds before the widget goes, `{time}` the round's clock, and `{name}` and `{score}` the player's own. They update by themselves.
- **Bars** show a set amount, a counter out of a goal you choose, or the time left. Split one into up to 20 pieces for lives or charges. Bars glide to new values.
- **Choices.** Each choice sends a signal with the player who picked it as the tank that set it off, so a shop can repair, reward or fling just that player. A menu closes for the player who picks unless it stays open. Clicking a choice never fires your gun.
- **Who sees it:** everyone, including players who join later, the players in the trigger's area, or only the player that set it off. *Hide after* takes it away after a few seconds.
- 16 slots per player. A widget in a slot replaces the one before it, so a score in its own slot never gets covered by a message.
- The inspector draws a picture of the screen with the widget on it, which replays the entrance as you change it. The logic preview shows widgets over the canvas, and you can click their choices there as the test tank.
- A new **UI** palette holds Message, Counter display, Choice menu and Hide UI.

## Tank that set it off

*Who* can now be **Tank that set it off**: only the tank behind what happened, wherever it is. That's the tank that crossed an area, the first tank inside a While inside area or the player who picked a choice, and it carries through signals, delays, counters and group watchers. A plate across the map can now reward just the tank that stepped on it.

## Scripts

- `tankyard.hud` widgets take `style`, `title`, `icon`, `panel`, `opacity`, `accent`, `enter`, `pulse`, `segments` and `choices`. A pick raises the new `choice` event in the pack that showed the widget, with the widget's ID as `name` and the choice, from 1, as `value`.
- Map JSON gains the `ui` and `hide_ui` actions and the `cause` value for `who`. See the [modding guide](../modding.md#ui).

## Performance

- Pushing a crate, door or other body online no longer lags behind your tank. The client moves the body with your predicted tank, as the server will, and draws it there until the server's next update agrees.
- Lights off screen no longer take the places of lights on screen, so a dark arena with many lamps keeps the ones you can see.
- Grid decor draws only the lines on screen, which made decor in large dark maps about twice as fast with software rendering.
- The server sends map widgets only when what a player sees changes, at most ten times a second, and players can pick at most ten times a second.
- Smoke-run frame timings include the time the graphics driver takes to draw each phase, so they point at real costs.

## Fixes

- Lamps no longer show a dark dot in the middle of their glow or a thin hard ring around it. The bulb is white-hot, with a soft glint.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.4.0 uses protocol 30, so 1.4.0 servers and players play only with each other. Maps and packs from earlier versions open and play unchanged; maps that use Show UI, Hide UI or *Tank that set it off* need 1.4.0.
