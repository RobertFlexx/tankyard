# Tank Yard v2.0.3

The bosses have started paying attention, Axiom finally has a body worth being afraid of, and the scoreboard folds up when you're tired of seeing who's beating you. It plays with 2.0.0 and 2.0.2, so nobody has to update at the same time.

## Bosses

- **They aim now.** Bosses lead a moving tank and, just before a special, line up on where you'll be when the warning ends. A sharp turn can still slip the shot, so dodging remains a valid career.
- **Straight lines.** They drive in clean legs from one heading to the next, closing in, backing off or cutting around you, instead of drifting about.
- **They notice when you're hurt.** A wounded tank is a more attractive target than it used to be. Sorry.
- **Bastion has a cannon.** Same hull, one heavy round with a proper warning. It's lighter than the Siege Engine's, so it ruins your day rather than ending it. Once Bastion is badly hurt, explosive charges bracket the line.
- A few bosses got small buffs, mostly quicker and meaner drones.

See [Bosses and learning](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.3/docs/bosses-and-learning.md).

## Axiom Worldshaper

- Rebuilt from the hull up on the glow engine: six working cannons, a turning gold and teal astrolabe, and attacks that look like they cost something.
- All twelve attacks sit on a dock with number keys, cooldowns and tooltips.
- Holding fire walks a stream of shells across the forward cannons, and they meet at your cursor.

## Scoreboard, party and chat

- **An arrow folds the scoreboard away,** and **L** does the same. It stays how you left it.
- **Each row shows that player's tank,** in miniature, so you know what's about to happen to you.
- With the tank menu open the board shortens to clear it, and your own row stays on it however the match is going.
- **Party rows show whose mic is off.** The silence now comes with an explanation.
- **Up and Down in chat** bring back what you already said, in case it was worth saying twice.

## Arena Workshop

- **Ctrl+F finds things** by kind, group, number or what a trigger does, and steps through the results.
- Drawing, moving and resizing show live measurements, and the status bar gives the size of your selection.
- Undo and redo say what they changed and flash it on the map.
- The Check list keeps itself up to date while you work.
- Copying with nothing selected no longer empties your clipboard. It was a very efficient way to lose a clipboard.

See [the map editor guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.3/docs/map-editor.md).

## Installing and speed

- The shell installer for Linux, macOS and FreeBSD now unpacks in your cache folder rather than `/tmp`, so a small or full temporary drive no longer stops an install. If extraction still fails, your current game is left alone.
- A busy match makes about a fifth fewer memory allocations per frame. The picture is the same.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 2.0.3 still uses **protocol 34**, so it plays with 2.0.0 and 2.0.2 clients and servers, and maps and packs carry over unchanged. Boss behavior and Axiom's cannons come from the server, so you'll see them offline and on servers running 2.0.3.
