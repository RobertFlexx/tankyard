# Tank Yard v1.3.0

Push doors and crates, paths that carry cover around the arena, darkness with real light, and bots that learn which tanks win. This release adds a physics layer to the Arena Workshop, a new built-in arena that uses it, and a trained driving network for bots. Everything in [v1.2.0](v1.2.0.md) is included.

## Physics in the workshop

- **Pushable bodies.** Turn any group into one solid body that tanks push by driving into it. Choose whether it slides any way, left and right, or up and down, how far it can go in each direction, its weight, a spring that brings it home, friction, bounce and whether shots knock it about. Its decor, pads and trigger areas ride along. Bodies stop at other cover and never crush a tank.
- **Paths.** Give a group a custom animation: a list of points it travels through, each with its own timing, wait, turn and curve, played once, on a loop or there and back. The canvas draws the path from where the group rests, with numbered points, a ghost of the group at the point you're editing and a dot riding it at its own pace.
- **New triggers.** **On touch** and **On release** watch a group's blocks arrive on or leave an area, so a crate on a plate can open a gate. **Shove body**, **Fling tanks**, **Blast** (outward, or inward with a negative speed), **Zoom view**, **Lighting** and **Play path** join the action list.
- **New pads.** **Ice** cuts grip so tanks slide, and a **Gravity well** draws tanks toward its middle.
- A new **Physics** palette holds ready-made pieces: Fling pad, Blast, Vortex, Crate plate, Plate release, Shove, Path, Zoom out, Nightfall, Lights on and Lightning.

## Darkness and light

- A map can start dark, tinted toward any color, and a **Lighting** trigger can change it during a round, over time and along a curve. **Go back after** returns it to what it was, which makes lightning flashes.
- Lights, the new **Spotlight** and **Beam**, particle effects with **Glow** on, hazard, portal, launch and repair pads, every tank and every shot light the floor around them. Without shader support, the darkness is a plain veil with soft light spots.
- Spotlights fade smoothly from the lamp to nothing, leave a soft pool where they land and shine from a flared lamp with glare on its lens. Lights have a halo and a glint, and beams glow at both ends with a brighter stretch drifting along them. With **Reduced motion** on, the stretch stays still.
- With **Reduced motion** on, sudden lighting changes ease in over about half a second.

## Decor and editing

- New decor shapes: Spotlight, Beam, Grid, Disc, Triangle and Hexagon, plus a **Roof** piece.
- **Near a tank** makes decor respond to your own tank: roofs that clear as you drive under them, signs that appear as you approach, and decor that grows or glows.
- Blocks of a pushable body are drawn as crates, with a frame, braces or slats and corner bolts, in the game and in the editor, so players can tell what gives way.
- Pads have bold corner brackets, and hazard and repair pads breathe slowly so they read as live from across the arena. With **Reduced motion** on, they hold still.
- Name groups. Names show in the Map tab's new group list and in every trigger summary, such as "hide group 4 (Vault gate)".
- The Map tab lists every group with what it holds and whether tanks push it, with buttons to make it start hidden, lock it while you work around it, or select all of it.
- Maps hold up to 512 pads, 2,048 decor items and 512 triggers.
- The Check tab notes a Shove body on a group that isn't pushable and a Play path with no points, with a link to each. They no longer stop a playtest.
- The [physics and lighting tutorial](../physics-tutorial.md) builds a dark yard with a push door, a crate puzzle, a ferry and a storm. The bundled **Night Works** pack is the finished map.

## New arena: Foundry

A dim, point-symmetric works floor for Free For All, team modes, Deathmatch and Sandbox. A furnace in the middle glows red and then blows tanks back every ten seconds. Conveyor belts run along the top and bottom, cranes ride back and forth on paths, and two press rooms hold repair pads behind push doors that only open inward.

## Bots

- Bots steer with a small neural network trained by self-play. Each tick it scores sixteen directions from what the bot sees along each one: walls, incoming shots, its target and other tanks. Across nine modes, network bots scored 15–55% more points a minute than bots using the older steering, died less in every mode except Co-op, and touched walls and stood still about as often. Beginner bots keep the plainer steering.
- Bots share what they learn: which tanks earn the most points per minute alive, which tanks beat which, and which upgrade paths lead to the strongest tanks. Upgrades, targets and caution follow it, more so at higher difficulties.
- That memory persists. Servers keep it in `bot_memory_file` (default `bot-memory.json`; `""` turns it off) and offline games in the data folder. Sandbox and modded matches don't teach it anything.

## Scripts and hooks

- New script commands: `play_map_path`, `set_map_lighting`, `shove_map_body` and `fling_tank`, and `map_body` to read where a body has been pushed.
- New hook actions: `map_path`, `map_light`, `map_shove` and `fling`.
- See the [modding guide](../modding.md#physics-and-lighting) for the map schema.

## Fixes

- Text no longer turns into black boxes after the window changes size in a dark arena or the workshop. Deleting a texture now clears it from the renderer's binding cache, so a font atlas that grows later is uploaded where it belongs.
- Pushed bodies keep pace with the tank pushing them, at its speed divided by the body's weight. Contact uses the same movement rule on the host and in prediction; nearby tanks cannot push across a gap, and cooperating pushers do not make a light body outrun them. Before, a tank lost contact each time the impact stopped it, and heavier crates crept along at about a third of the tank's speed.
- **Select** in the Map tab's group list switches to Edit, so the group can be dragged right away, and opens its settings from the top.
- Palette hints for pieces near the right of the dock stay clear of the inspector.
- Client prediction moves sliding bodies forward while replaying inputs, so pushing a body online doesn't stutter.
- Game data sent to players always leaves out map triggers and paths, which the server runs, so a map with logic can't produce data a client can't read.
- The Check tab no longer says a group nothing moves when a path moves it.
- Light cones, beams and glints use fixed mesh storage instead of allocating each frame. Lighting clipped outside the window no longer darkens the whole screen.
- Roof stripes keep their angle and spacing on tall or wide pieces instead of bunching into a dark wedge.
- A damaged bot-memory file cannot stall startup by acting as a named pipe.
- Release packages include Night Works and Switchyard, with both checked alongside the other example packs.
- Connectivity checks ignore bodies and groups that triggers hide or move, so maps with push doors and gates validate.

## Compatibility

Protocol **29**. Tank Yard 1.3.0 clients and servers play only with each other; update both together. Clients from 1.2.0 and earlier receive an update message. Maps and packs from earlier versions load unchanged, and maps that use the new pieces need 1.3.0.
