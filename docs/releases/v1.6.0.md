# Tank Yard v1.6.0

This version adds the Gatewright tank, lets you host your own saved maps and map packs, and adds many more ways to style UI widgets. Everything in [v1.5.2](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/releases/v1.5.2.md) is included.

## Gatewright

Gatewright is a new level-55 tank. It upgrades from Switchback, Wisp, Undertow or Meteor.

- Its ability, Rift pair, has an 18-second cooldown. After you activate it, your next two shots within 8 seconds open rifts where they land: a violet entry first, then a teal exit.
- You, your teammates and any bullet that enters the entry come out of the exit, moving in the same direction at the same speed. Opponent tanks, drones, traps, mines and flag carriers can't use rifts.
- A pair stays open for 15 seconds. The two rifts open at least 216 units apart. If a shot lands where a rift can't open, such as inside a wall or too close to another rift, you get the shot back.
- Activating the ability again, dying or switching tanks closes your pair. Moving walls close any rift they cover.
- The HUD shows which rift your next shot opens and how long the pair has left. Each rift shows a field of stars that shifts as the camera moves.
- Bots don't use rifts.

See [Gatewright](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/playing.md#gatewright) in the playing guide.

## Host your own maps

- The host page has a new **Change map…** button. It opens a picker with three tabs: Arenas, My maps (maps saved in the Arena Workshop) and Map packs (packs in your mods library).
- When the server starts, a saved map is packed and sent to players like any other pack map. If a saved map can't be opened, the picker says why and has buttons for the editor and the maps folder.
- **Browse for a pack…** opens your file browser to add a `.zip` pack or a pack folder's `mod.json`. A pack that still needs a review opens in Mods first.
- Saved maps and pack maps host at their own size (Arena size → Map's own). Adaptive borders are off for them, so walls stay where you placed them.

See [Choosing the map](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/hosting.md#choosing-the-map).

## Widget styles

Show UI widgets and script widgets have new style options:

- Shape: rounded, square, cut corners, slanted, tag or ribbon
- Border: line, none, thick, double, dashed or corner brackets
- Fill: solid, gradient, glass, stripes, grid or dots, with an optional second color
- Lettering: clean, shadow, block, outline or glow, plus capital letters and a separate title color
- Bars: capsule, flat, thin, striped, glow or pips
- Choice buttons: capsule, box, outline or underline

The workshop has twelve preset looks: Theme, Neon, Arcade, Parchment, Terminal, Royal, Glass, Hazard, Sticker, Frost, Ember and Jungle. A look changes a widget's colors and style and keeps its text, position and choices.

Scripts use the new `tankyard.hud` fields `shape`, `border`, `fill`, `fill_color`, `lettering`, `caps`, `title_color`, `bar_style` and `buttons`. Map files only store the options a widget uses. See [modding](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/modding.md#hud-widgets) and the [workshop UI guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/map-editor.md#ui).

## Other changes

- When the same sound starts several times in the same instant, it now plays once at the loudest volume instead of stacking.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.6.0 uses **protocol 32**. Update clients, servers and separate relays together; 1.5.0 to 1.5.2 use protocol 31. Older maps and packs still open. Maps that use the new widget styles need 1.6.0.
