# Tank Yard v1.6.0

A new tank that opens rifts across the arena, hosting straight from your own saved maps and map packs, and widget looks that let every arena's interface have its own style. Everything in [v1.5.2](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/releases/v1.5.2.md) is included.

## Gatewright

- **A rift gun at level 55**, from Switchback, Wisp, Undertow or Meteor. Activate **Rift pair** (18-second cooldown), then fire twice within 8 seconds: the first shot opens a violet entry where it lands, the second a teal exit.
- **You, your team and every round** that goes into the entry come out of the exit with the same heading and speed. Opponents, drones, traps, mines and flag carriers stay on their side.
- **Each rift is a window into deep space:** layered stars and nebulae that drift with the camera at different depths, a living rim and a thread between the two ends for you and your team. A pair stays open for 15 seconds, then each end eases shut and pops.
- **No dead ends:** a shot that finds no room (in a wall, too close to the other end or to another rift) fizzles and gives its charge back. Pairs open at least 216 units apart, so a trip always reads as a jump rather than a slide. Moving walls crush rifts they cover, and a new activation, death or a tank change closes your pair.
- The HUD shows which end you fire next and how long your pair stays open. Rifts have their own short sounds, and identical sounds that start together play once at the louder volume.

See [Gatewright](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/playing.md#gatewright) in the playing guide.

## Host your own maps

- **Change map…** on the host page opens a picker with three tabs: **Arenas**, **My maps** (maps saved in the Arena Workshop) and **Map packs** (packs in your mods library).
- A saved map is packed for the server when it starts, so players receive it like any pack map. Maps that no longer open show why, with shortcuts to the editor and the maps folder.
- **Browse for a pack…** opens your system's file browser to add a `.zip` pack or a pack folder's `mod.json`; **Open mods folder** shows the library. A pack that still needs review opens in **Mods** first.
- Saved and pack maps host at their own size (**Arena size → Map's own**) with adaptive borders off, so walls stay where you placed them.

See [Choosing the map](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/hosting.md#choosing-the-map).

## Widget looks

- **Shapes:** rounded, square, cut corners, slanted, tag and ribbon panels.
- **Borders:** line, none, thick, double, dashed and corner brackets.
- **Fills:** solid, gradient, glass with a sheen, stripes, grid and dots, with their own second color.
- **Lettering:** clean, shadow, painted block, outline or glow, capital letters and a title color of its own.
- **Bars and buttons:** capsule, flat, thin, striped, glowing or pip bars; capsule, box, outline or underline choices.
- **Twelve one-click looks** in the workshop (Theme, Neon, Arcade, Parchment, Terminal, Royal, Glass, Hazard, Sticker, Frost, Ember and Jungle) restyle a widget at once and leave its words, place and choices alone.

Scripts use the same options in `tankyard.hud`: `shape`, `border`, `fill`, `fill_color`, `lettering`, `caps`, `title_color`, `bar_style` and `buttons`. Map files leave out options a widget doesn't use. See [modding](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/modding.md#hud-widgets) and the [workshop UI guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.6.0/docs/map-editor.md#ui).

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.6.0 uses **protocol 32**. Update clients, servers and separate relays together; 1.5.0 to 1.5.2 use protocol 31. Earlier maps and packs still open. Maps that use the new widget looks need 1.6.0.
