# Tank Yard v0.11.1

A cleaner macOS installer. Everything in [v0.11.0](v0.11.0.md) is included.

## macOS installer

- The Installer window shows a small Tank Yard emblem in the corner of its sidebar instead of the whole app icon blown up behind the step list. It is sharp on Retina displays and fits both the light and the dark Installer theme.
- The welcome and summary pages are laid out in short sections and stay readable in dark mode. The Terminal command for the dedicated server no longer sits in an unreadable white box, and can be pasted as is.

## Installers

- When github.com's download links keep failing, `install.sh`, `install-macos.sh` and `install.ps1` switch to downloading the same files through the GitHub API after two tries, and use it for the rest of the install. Checksums are verified the same way.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x and v0.11.x clients and servers play together.
