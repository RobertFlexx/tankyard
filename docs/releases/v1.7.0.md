# Tank Yard v1.7.0

This version adds boards, so map makers can build control panels, keypads and whole computers out of UI. It also raises the limits on map logic, gives the Zoom view trigger more control, and fixes Gatewright's rifts. Everything in [v1.6.0](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.7.0/docs/releases/v1.6.0.md) is included.

## Boards

A Show UI widget can now be a board: a grid of up to 32 by 32 squares holding up to 128 cells.

- Cells can be labels, buttons, screens, seven-segment digits, lights or meters.
- A button sends its signal when clicked, and the player who clicked is the tank that set it off. A board stays up after a click, and each player can click up to 20 times a second.
- Buttons and lights light up from a counter, one bit of a counter, or a group while it's shown. Meters fill from a counter; tall meters fill from the bottom up.
- Every cell fills in counter fields as the counters change, so a Digits cell can show a twelve-digit number and a Screen can show a byte in binary.
- The Arena Workshop has a board designer. Click a square to add a cell, drag a cell to move it, and drag its corner to resize it. **Copy cell** numbers keys for you, **Add number keys** lays out a keypad with a free channel for each key, and **Add 8 bit lights** shows a counter's bits. Each cell has its own words, text size, alignment and colors, and the board's buttons and meters take the widget's button and bar looks.
- The logic preview shows boards over the canvas, and clicking a button there sends its signal as the test tank.
- Scripts show boards with the new `board` field of `tankyard.hud`. A click on a button raises the pack's `choice` event with the button's place in `cells`.

See [Boards](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.7.0/docs/map-editor.md#boards) in the workshop guide and the [boards tutorial](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.7.0/docs/boards-tutorial.md), which builds a switch panel that opens a vault.

## Bigger logic

- Maps have 255 counters (up from 8) and 255 signal channels (up from 32).
- Counters hold whole numbers up to ±1,073,741,823 (up from ±99,999).
- Calculate has bit math, AND, OR, XOR, shift left and shift right, and a power.
- Calculate can move its counter, or the other counter, along by another counter's value. One trigger can then work through a list of counters.
- Counter fields have formats: `{3:,}` adds commas, `{3:04}` pads with zeros, `{3:b}` and `{3:x}` show binary and hex, and `{3|2|1:4}` joins counters into one long number.
- A map can hold 4,096 triggers (up from 512) and 4,096 decor items (up from 2,048). Each tick allows 4,096 chained actions (up from 256), and 4,096 delayed actions can wait at once.
- The workshop stays quick with thousands of triggers: logic links, the loop check and the canvas arrows only look at triggers that can be linked. The Logic tab shows counters 1 to 8 and any other counter the map uses or that isn't 0.
- Packs can be larger: archives of up to 16 MiB with files of up to 4 MiB, and up to 4 MiB of game data.

See [Counters](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.7.0/docs/map-editor.md#counters).

## Zoom view

- **Hold until the next zoom** keeps a zoom until another Zoom view changes it.
- **Change over** sets how long the view takes to change, 0.1 to 10 seconds, along a curve.
- **Turn toward the area** moves the camera from the tank toward the middle of the trigger's area, up to centering it there.
- A new zoom starts from wherever the view is, so chained zooms no longer jump. A destroyed tank gets its usual view back.
- Servers now send everything a zoomed-out or turned view can show.

## Gatewright

- Fixed rifts letting a tank travel through them only once a round on maps without pads. Tanks can now use a rift pair as often as it stays open.
- Rifts have new sounds in place of the old hissing ones: a swell with bells climbing over it when a rift opens, a quick warp when something goes through, a soft pop when a rift collapses, and two low notes when a shot finds no room for one.

## Other changes

- Script boards and their cells take colors as `"#rrggbb"` or `{ r, g, b }`, like other widget fields.
- The workshop plugin helpers `editor.free_channel` and `editor.free_counter` cover all 255 channels and counters, and skip the channels of choices and board buttons and the counters in bars, cells and text.
- The workshop no longer draws a link from a motion without a group to an On arrive trigger without one.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.7.0 uses **protocol 33**. Update clients, servers and separate relays together; 1.6.0 uses protocol 32. Older maps and packs still open. Maps that use boards, more than 8 counters or 32 channels, the new math, counter formats or zoom settings, or more than 512 triggers need 1.7.0.
