# Tank Yard v0.11.8

Mods page fixes, and a quick way to turn off packs that are gone. Everything in [v0.11.7](v0.11.7.md) is included.

## Mods

- When an enabled pack has been deleted, the message about it now has a **Disable?** switch that turns it off for good.
- A pack that's installed but can't be read now says it "cannot load" instead of "no longer installed", and the same switch turns it off.
- Pack icons and on/off switches scroll with their rows.
- Scrolling over the pack list scrolls only the list, not the whole page with it. The lobby list and party chat work the same way.
- The page no longer scrolls behind the safety review or the file browser.

## Menus

- On Macs, the scrolling area of each page was clipped upside down, cutting off the bottom of lists and showing buttons above the header. Pages, the tank tree, the lobby list and the server console now clip where they should.
- Tank and boss previews on the title screen, Practice, Settings and Credits scroll with their pages.
- The Bastion badge on the title screen moves up above any message instead of hiding behind it.

## Games

- After a death, the camera always switches to your killer. In a later game it could stay on your wreck instead, depending on how often you died in the game before.
- The killer card no longer replays when the server sends new content while you're down.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x and v0.11.x clients and servers play together.
