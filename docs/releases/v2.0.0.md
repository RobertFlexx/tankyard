# Tank Yard v2.0.0

The biggest update Tank Yard has had. All fourteen bosses are redrawn and fight harder, tanks are rebuilt and rebalanced, the Arena Workshop gains layers, map logic gains real gates and circuits, and parties are much better to play in. Everything in [v1.7.0](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.0/docs/releases/v1.7.0.md) is included.

## Bosses

- **New looks.** Every boss is redrawn in clean, simple shapes, each with a glowing core that brightens as it charges and pulses when it's nearly beaten.
- **Attacks you can read.** Before a special attack, a boss shows where its rounds will go: lanes for a fan, spokes for a ring and chevrons for a charge. Lances, darts, shells and charges each have their own look and glow.
- **The Siege Engine's cannon.** It winds up for 1.5 seconds and draws a red line to the first wall in its way, then fires a huge, very fast round that destroys any tank it reaches, shields included. Get off the line or behind cover. Walls stop it, spawn protection holds, and a perfect parry sends it back as a heavy shot.
- **Fights that fit the arena.** A boss's health follows how many players are in the arena and how high their levels are. It moves with them if they join or leave, keeping how hurt the boss is. Bosses with swarms launch fewer drones against fewer players, and reach their full swarm against four.
- **Shared rewards.** Everyone who hit a boss in the last minute shares its reward.
- **Smarter help.** High-level bots now join a boss fight while they're healthy enough for it, and shoot down the drones that chase them.
- A warned attack always comes, even if its target ducks behind cover.

See [Fighting bosses in 2.0](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.0/docs/bosses-and-learning.md#fighting-bosses-in-20).

## Tanks

- **21 new layouts.** Bastion, Catapult, Commodore, Cyclone Gunner, Drone Sentinel, Fabricator, Fortress Array, Gunship, Interceptor, Javelin, Monolith, Octo Sprayer, Palisade, Pinball, Quarry, Railstorm, Rotator, Spectral Guard, Starburst, Switchback and Tri-Sniper have cleaner, balanced shapes, with turrets on proper mounts.
- **Rebalanced from thousands of bot duels.** 65 tanks changed.
- **The strongest came down.** Reprisal and Railstorm deal about half the damage they did, and both fire more slowly. Reprisal also has less health and a longer ability cooldown. Mirage lost 19%, and Ranger, Capacitor, Overcharger, Catapult, Riposte, Counterguard, Sniper, Stalker and others lost up to 15%.
- **The weakest came up.** Rook, Pinball and Bastille, the weakest level-55 tanks, gained 26 to 33%: Bastille lays traps faster and Rook places turrets sooner. Machine Gun gained 15%, and Destroyer, Annihilator, Spread Shot, Fighter, Triple Shot, Octo Tank, Tri-Angle and others gained up to 9%.

## Arena Workshop

- **Layers.** Every object sits on a layer from 0 to 99. The layer bar and the **,** and **.** keys pick a layer (hold Shift to skip empty ones). Everything else fades and can't be selected, so you can reach what's underneath. Layers are only for building and never change how a map plays.
- **Reach what's stacked.** Clicking the one selected object again selects the next object under the pointer, top to bottom, and wraps round.
- **The Links view (L) shows every connection.** Busy channels and counters gather at a hub so a large circuit stays readable, and drawing it is much cheaper on low-end computers.

See [Layers](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.0/docs/map-editor.md#layers).

## Map logic

- **More gates.** Alongside All, Any, Not all, None and One, a gate can now open on an even number of true conditions (XNOR, or NOT with one condition), an odd number (parity) or most of them (majority). A trigger can check eight conditions, up from four.
- **Live gates.** **On input change** runs a trigger whenever a counter or group its conditions read changes, and **Gate to counter** writes the gate's result to a counter. Together they make gates that follow their inputs, and a chain of them settles within a tick.
- **A Circuits palette.** Ready-made live gates, sum and carry bits, latches, comparators, registers, numbers and pulses. The Gates palette adds NOT, NAND, NOR, XNOR, majority and parity.
- **Faster and larger.** **Every** can run as often as every 0.05 seconds. Each tick allows 32,768 chained actions and 262,144 condition checks, and 16,384 delayed actions can wait at once. Boards follow counters within two ticks.

See [Live gates](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.0/docs/map-editor.md#live-gates).

## Parties

- The party rail shows each member's health, turns grey when they're down, and says when someone joins or leaves.
- On a team, arrows at the edge of the screen point toward allies out of view, with their names.
- The party panel lists each member's level, what they're doing, their tank and their health.
- The leader can remove a member with **Kick**, confirmed with a second click.

See [In a match](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.0/docs/parties.md#in-a-match).

## Gatewright

- An opponent's rifts now look different from yours: crimson and amber, ringed with thorns. Your own side's stay violet and teal.
- Rounds glide through rifts instead of jumping or streaking across the arena.

## Fixes

- Clicks no longer pass through one panel to what's under it. Clicking a tank upgrade over the party icon, for example, now only picks the upgrade.
- Co-op boss waves are tougher, and grow more with the size of the party.
- A window now always draws at its own size, so it stays sharp when maximized and runs faster when it's smaller than the chosen resolution. Resolution still sets the render size in fullscreen, and Render scale works in both.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 2.0.0 uses **protocol 34**. Update clients, servers and separate relays together; 1.7.0 uses protocol 33. Older maps and packs still open. Maps that use the new gates, more than four conditions, On input change, Gate to counter or timers under 0.25 seconds need 2.0.0.
