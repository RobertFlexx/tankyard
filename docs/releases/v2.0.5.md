# Tank Yard v2.0.5

A new boss that would prefer you stayed far away, parries that hit back, and a boss bar that finally looks like part of the game.

## Railgunner

- The biggest regular boss, with a lot of health and a heavier cannon than Siege Engine's. It leads moving targets, hunts whoever is furthest away, and is at its worst when you're right under the barrel.
- Its barrel is the warning. A rail shot only strains the latches. When the plasma beam is coming, the armor splits, the coils spread and the muzzle claws unfold, and the whole thing throws open as its aim locks.
- The beam sweeps after its target, then the boss vents with its barrel still open and takes 40% more damage. That's your window.
- It hops backward when crowded and keeps weak flak guns for anyone who gets close.
- It turns up very rarely on its own, and only in arenas with enough experienced players. You can also pick it in a custom Boss Rush, summon it as staff, or practice in **Get under the rail**.

## Parries that hit back

- Parry the beam and it breaks. The stream stops, a bright clash goes off at your tank, and the strike runs back up the line for 10% of the boss's full health.
- Railgunner's cannon goes back as an ion strike (9%), Siege Engine's shell as fire and a blast (7%), and Bastion's slug as a smaller steel-blue hit (4%). Railgunner's lighter echo round is worth half a cannon clash.
- Each clash has its own look, sound and light. One volley can only clash once, and bots deal 40% of what a player does.
- Reduced motion and Effects at zero keep the headline and damage number without the flashes, particles or camera shake. Attack warnings stay visible.

## Boss effects

- Trails behave like gas left hanging in the air. They stay where they were laid, drift on the wind and spread out as they fade, rather than following the round around like a sticker.
- Bosses leave engine exhaust from where their engines actually are.
- Fire no longer shows faint stripes, and the flame stream holds together in flight.
- The plasma beam curves where it bends instead of folding at the joints.
- The boss bar and the **BOSS INCOMING** and **BOSS DEFEATED** banners are redrawn. The bar shows what the boss is doing, and turns red when it's after you.

## Staff tools and fixes

- Server staff get a searchable player list, masked ban review and lifting, live server health, and boss summon and dismiss controls. Every action uses the server's existing permissions.
- Lifting a ban restores the address ban if its linked account record can't be saved. Reading server health doesn't fill the audit log.
- Creator tools add boss-health presets, heal-all and jumping beside a living player. Reason and duration presets, unmute and clearer player details improve moderation.
- Fixed the Mods row switch losing its click to the row beneath it. The detail button still works.
- Fixed Unlock becoming unclickable after focusing the password field. Show/Hide and keypad Enter work there too.

Signed staff packs stay portable. Existing packs unlock the updated client, and their passwords and identities don't change.

## Security

- Private server passwords can only be guessed a limited number of times per address, with more room for shared relay addresses.
- One address can no longer fill the party relay's rooms or a WebSocket relay's connections and lock everyone else out.
- Names and chat can't be made of invisible characters, so nobody turns up as a blank on the leaderboard or as a perfect copy of someone else's name.

## Compatibility

This release stays on **protocol 34**, so 2.0 clients and servers keep playing together.

- Railgunner, clashes and the new staff tools come from the server, which needs to run 2.0.5 to have them.
- A 2.0.4 client on a 2.0.5 server sees Railgunner as a very large Siege Engine and a clash as an ordinary parry. Clients older than 2.0.4 still have the boss-timer disconnect that release fixed, so they should update.
- A saved map that names Railgunner needs a 2.0.5 host.

Existing map packs, Luau scripts, settings and staff packs carry over.
