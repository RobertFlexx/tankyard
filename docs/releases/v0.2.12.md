Ten new tanks, a level 55 upgrade tier, and tighter combat controls.

- Riposte now parries for 0.18 seconds. A hit returns a much stronger homing shot and heals 8% of maximum health. Its normal cannon still deals 18 damage.
- Riposte upgrades to Counterguard at level 45, then Reprisal at 55. Their windows tighten to 0.16 / 0.14 seconds, with stronger returns and 12% healing. Reprisal adds two short-lived echo shots. Returned shots keep the existing steering and travel limits.
- Every level-45 class has a level-55 upgrade. The nine final-tier tanks are Reprisal, Fusillade, Longbow, Bastille, Commodore, Harrier, Juggernaut, Mirage, and Rook, bringing the catalog to 102 tanks.
- Level 55 needs 72,734 XP and earns 38 skill points. Progression through level 45 keeps the previous curve. Each stat still caps at seven points.
- A small bar below your tank shows when its next manual shot is ready. Charge weapons show charge while held. Online bars use local prediction so they respond when you fire. Authoritative deployable counts prevent false recoil when a trap or turret pool is full.
- First launch offers Dark and Light side by side, using the actual game renderer. The choice is saved; existing settings are respected.
- Spawn protection lasts up to 30 seconds. It pulses white subtly and ends on movement or attack.
- Traps launch farther before settling. Trap Range upgrades their launch distance without extending their lifetime. Ranger reloads a little slower.
- Smasher bots intercept, flank heavy cannons, close in for contact, and retreat when hurt. Parry bots wait for an incoming shot to reach their window. Normal reaction delays and player stats still apply.
- Minions hold a stable formation around the cursor, keep targets through small distance changes, and wait until a shot can reach. Recall no longer rotates the swarm continuously. Passive minion replacements no longer push the owner around. Idle drones respect cover when selecting targets.
- Input packets repeat two recent frames to recover short clicks and ability taps after packet loss. Repeated frames are ignored after use. Queues and receive work remain bounded, and bursts do not kick players.

Includes Linux x86-64 / ARM64, universal macOS, and Windows x86-64 binaries, installers, and logo assets.

Update and restart servers as well as clients. This release uses protocol 10.
