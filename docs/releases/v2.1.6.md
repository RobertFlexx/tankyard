# Tank Yard v2.1.6

Two rare bosses join Boss Rush. Pick **Game modes → Boss Rush → Bosses** to fight either one immediately, or meet them as rare arrivals in a strong arena.

- **Jester** opens with a recorded laugh and a three-card trick. Its porcelain comedy mask sits above a turning gun carriage, and three brass bells swing from a rainbow cap. It borrows the other bosses’ weapons and Axiom’s combat abilities. The boss bar announces each act, and its rainbow shots follow the borrowed weapon’s pattern. Sunderworm keeps its attacks to itself.
- **Carillon** is a traveling bell foundry. On the hammer peal, dodge the fast slug and the explosive following it. Its molten fans and mine ring each leave a way through. Only Sunderworm has a tougher hull or a rarer natural arrival.
- **Siege Engine** can turn its gun independently of its tracks. The cannon follows a moving target until the final 0.6 seconds of its warning. **Prism** has a little more health, and its primary shots fly faster and hit slightly harder.

Hosts can use `jester` and `carillon` in a Boss Rush lineup, a script or a map trigger. Protocol **35** supports earlier 2.1.x clients. Update the host to run the encounters and the client to see and hear their full presentation.
