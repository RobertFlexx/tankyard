# Tank Yard v1.5.1

Arcade Hall's vault lamp turns green on time and its waypoint stays accurate. Fading signs stay over what they mark, and the workshop keeps group numbers readable on large maps. Everything in [v1.5.0](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.5.1/docs/releases/v1.5.0.md) is included.

## Arcade Hall

- The vault lamp turns green when the gate opens at 0:40, as the pack's guide says. Its tint trigger had no strength, and a tint at 0% clears the color instead of painting it.
- The shop's **Vault guide** reads **Opens at 0:40** before the vault opens and **Gate open** afterwards. Two signs share slot 30, each gated by **Round time**. The [menus and waypoints tutorial](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.5.1/docs/ui-tutorial.md#point-toward-the-vault) now shows how.

## Interfaces in the arena

- A sign or tank marker that fades out stays over its area or tank while the view moves, instead of sliding across the screen with the camera. If its target is already gone, it disappears at once.

## Workshop

- Group numbers no longer pile up on large maps. One number stands for several nearby objects of the same group, numbers make way for trigger tags, and selected or pointed-at objects show theirs first. Pointing at an object still names its group.
- Hover cards fit their title when there is nothing more to say, instead of always opening a wide box.
- Smoke runs of the workshop (`--page editor --smoke-frames N`) report the time spent on input, the preview, each canvas layer, the top bar, the dock and the inspector.

## Modding

- Hand-written **Tanks inside** and **Round time** conditions no longer need an `index`, so the [modding guide's](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.5.1/docs/modding.md) examples, such as `{ "kind": "time", "compare": "at_least", "value": 300 }`, load as printed. Counter and group conditions still name theirs. Tank Yard 1.5.0 and earlier want `"index": 0` on the others.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 1.5.1 keeps **protocol 31**, so it plays with 1.5.0 clients, servers and relays. Maps and packs from 1.5.0 open unchanged, and Arcade Hall 1.1.1 still opens in 1.5.0.
