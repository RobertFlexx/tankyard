# Tank Yard v2.1.0

Harder boss fights, animated health bars, new recorded sound mixes and a rare encounter beneath the arena. Everything in [v2.0.5](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/docs/releases/v2.0.5.md) is included.

**Multiplayer uses protocol 35. Update the host and all players together.** Clients and servers from 2.0 cannot play with 2.1.0. Existing settings, maps, Luau scripts and signed staff packs carry over.

## Boss fights

- Bosses choose between three special attacks according to range, nearby tanks and their target's movement. Each kind has its own priorities, remembers recent attackers and avoids piling onto the same player as every other boss.
- Later phases increase the pressure and shorten openings. Dangerous attacks still have visible warnings, and cover, sharp turns and defensive abilities remain useful.
- Railgunner's cannon and beam require a parry within **75 milliseconds** of pressing the ability while facing the incoming shot, within about **49 degrees**. Its smaller guns keep the normal parry window.
- Rail, plasma, burning shells and steel slugs have distinct clash effects and sounds. The rail clash has a heavier impact.

## Health bars and effects

- Boss health bars blend their colors smoothly, animate incoming damage and leave a glowing white damage trail. A red outline pulses and glows when the boss targets you.
- The headline bar keeps health, phase markers and attack messages visible while the boss moves off screen. Reduced Motion keeps the health information without the decorative flashes and shake.
- Projectile ribbons use finer curves near the camera. Engine exhaust holds its direction through stops and turns.
- Boss arrivals, weapons, parries, rifts and map sound cues use new mixes of recorded metal, stone, steam and creature performances. The recordings are CC0; credits and source details are in the [sound bank reference](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/assets/audio/foley/README.md).

<summary>Secret encounter: Sunderworm</summary>

Sunderworm is a long, segmented boss with a ground-shaking entrance. Cracks spread before it breaks through the floor, throwing tiles, earth and dust into the air. Its body can be hit only where it is above ground.

- Five phases introduce lunges, bone fans, bile, burrowing, larvae and spine waves. Watch the strike lane and eruption ring before moving in.
- At **16% health**, it tears into a head and tail that fight independently. A surviving half becomes more aggressive. Both must be destroyed.
- Its jaws unfold around hooked teeth, with a contracting throat and saliva strands. Blood streams break into falling droplets, pool on the floor and dry. Torn flesh, debris and blood use the arena's effects and lighting.
- Its health bar shows phase boundaries and warnings before lunges, eruptions and the split. A half's destruction is distinguished from the end of the encounter.
- An eligible natural boss arrival has about a **one-in-two-million** chance of being Sunderworm and needs an arena worth three full-level tanks. The Boss Rush roster rolls a **one-in-24,000** chance when opened. A selected encounter stays in the saved lineup.
- Hosts can explicitly choose `sunderworm` in a Boss Rush lineup or a map Boss trigger. Presentation packs can keep its local roster card available with `"menu": { "bosses": ["sunderworm"] }`.

## Stability and performance

- Players joining or reconnecting during a worm fight receive its real body positions. Visible segments and collision shapes stay together, and DLSS motion follows each segment's silhouette.
- Bots aim at nearby surfaced body segments and avoid them even when the head is far away.
- A large finishing hit no longer skips the worm's death effects. Exploding rounds cannot trigger their owner's death scene.
- Destroying both halves in the same tick fires encounter-completion logic and the Boss Rush team bonus once. Whole worms reserve room for their tails within the encounter limit.
- Blood, debris and sound queues have fixed limits. Sounds are prepared and cached for playback, and clients receive body positions without the host's full movement history or decision state.

## Updating a server

Install the **2.1.0 server and client together**, along with any separately deployed relay. Protocol 35 is incompatible with protocol 34 from 2.0.0–2.0.5; the connection screen explains which side needs an update.

See [hosting](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/docs/hosting.md), [Pi and Docker upgrades](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/docs/central-pi-upgrade.md), [bosses](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/docs/bosses-and-learning.md) and [modding](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.0/docs/modding.md).
