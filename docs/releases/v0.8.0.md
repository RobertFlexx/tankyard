# Tank Yard v0.8.0

Change modes with your party, and defend a real team home.

- **Party modes** in the main menu starts a new round on the existing host. Keep the invitation, members, chat and local voice mutes while changing mode, map, difficulty, team slots, starting level, arena size or adaptive borders. Only the authenticated leader can apply changes.
- Add an outbound internet relay route for private parties. It works around blocked inbound UDP, double NAT and CGNAT while the leader's computer still runs the match. Invites retain their IP endpoint and fall back to direct UDP if the relay is unavailable. Editing the invite endpoint keeps existing members connected.
- Separate party-host ownership from invite entry with challenge-bound signatures. Bound rooms, guests, queues, packet rates, handshake deadlines and idle connections; keep UDP tunnel destinations on the leader's loopback server.
- Replace small team-home circles with full-height two-team side strips and four-team corner bases. Share geometry between spawning, damage protection, map cover, tank effects and the minimap; follow animated arena bounds without changing the grid.
- Protect tanks whose whole hull is inside their own base. Protection ends at the boundary; stationary fifteen-second spawn immunity remains separate. Infection, co-op and Boss Rush retain their mode rules without permanent safe zones.
- Clear cover and new shape spawns from bases. Bots, boss drones and automatic targeting avoid protected defenders.
- Show clearer UDP connection failures and wrap menu messages within their reserved space. Correct successful restart acknowledgement handling so the leader actually enters the new round.

Protocol **20**: update clients, regular servers and relays together. Automatic relay invites through `tankyard.kokonico.me` work once the main server's relay runs v0.8.0. See [party hosting](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v0.8.0/docs/parties.md). Direct internet invites still need a working UDP forward and host firewall exception.
