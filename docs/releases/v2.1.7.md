# Tank Yard v2.1.7

Carillon reads moving targets more closely and brings six specials to a fight. Its hammer peal, molten pour and casting floor are joined by three new attacks.

- **Bellfall** closes three pairs of hot shots around a predicted lane. The final pair brings an explosive down the middle.
- **Blowdown** vents two glowing steam fronts, with the same two escape lanes in both. Listen for the second release.
- **Slag seekers** curve in from the flanks while two explosive shells follow the marked lanes.

Bastion gains **Crossfire**, three parryable slugs fired left, right and center, and **Shield wall**, a pair of trap fans with a route through the middle. Defender has about 8% more health and slightly faster, stronger fire. The Jester can imitate all five new specials in its own colors.

Boss Rush on Orrery Chamber now starts tanks throughout the arena. When a map provides no applicable spawn region, the server looks across the arena for room away from cover, hazards and nearby threats. Team bases and authored spawn regions take priority. Workshop maps can leave spawn regions empty on 2.1.7 and later.

Protocol **35** still supports earlier 2.1.x clients in standard arenas. Update the host for the new attacks and spawn placement. Update the client for their full warnings, lighting and sound.
