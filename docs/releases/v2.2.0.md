# Tank Yard v2.2.0

## Bankshot and skills

**Bankshot** branches from Tri-Angle at level 45. Its spring-mounted cannon kicks the hull backward. Fire near a wall or the arena edge and let the hull bank off it. The new **Recoil** skill (9) gives weapons more kick. The existing point budget still applies.

Hold a skill's **+** to spend points repeatedly, or hold **Shift** to fill it at once. Press **U** or click the panel's arrow to fold the skills away and keep your unspent point count visible.

## Bosses

Public arenas show the boss health bar with its phase markers, damage trail, and targeting glow. When several bosses are active, the bar gives priority to one pursuing you.

Bosses can plot a route around connected walls. Lose one behind cover and it will check your last visible position, then sweep nearby exits based on the heading it saw.

## Hosting and platform fixes

- Verified central owners can turn on **Invulnerability** under **Server → My tank** for their own tank that round.
- QuadNext keeps drags working when the pointer leaves a Windows window, stops stale key repeats on Wayland, and keeps held controls when a Mac window moves.

**Multiplayer uses protocol 36.** Update the game, server, and any separately deployed relay together. Releases 2.1.0–2.1.8 use protocol 35. See the [hosting guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.2.0/docs/hosting.md) for upgrade instructions.
