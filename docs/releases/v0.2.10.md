Homing rounds are easier to dodge and expire sooner.

- Steering is limited to about 63 degrees per second. Rounds only acquire targets within a 120-degree forward cone and 600 units, so missed shots cannot turn around and chase you again.
- Homing fire lasts at most 2.2 seconds or 850 units of travel. Automatic homing turrets have a 1.8-second lifetime. Bullet speed upgrades respect the travel limit.
- Riposte returns use the same steering and flight limits. Parrying an already homing round does not refresh its remaining lifetime.

Damage and reload rates are unchanged. This covers Stalker, Ranger, Phantom, Spectral Guard, Catapult, Auto 3, Auto 5, and reflected rounds. Drone controls are unchanged.

Update and restart your server to apply the balance changes online. Protocol 9 is unchanged.
