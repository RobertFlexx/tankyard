# Tank Yard v1.0.0

Tank Yard 1.0 is here: sliding doors, lifts and moving cover glide in the workshop, lights glow like real lamps, particles gain new shapes, and tanks and bosses look sharper.

Thank you to everyone who played, hosted, built maps and reported bugs on the way here.

## Motions

- Four new trigger actions animate a group. **Move group** glides it by a distance, and **Move group to** glides it to a place measured from where the map puts it. **Turn decor** and **Fade decor** turn or fade its decor. A group's blocks, pads, decor and trigger areas move together, and moving cover pushes tanks, shapes and bosses out of its way.
- Each motion has a length, one of eleven easing curves (Linear, Sine, Quad, Cubic, Quart, Quint, Expo, Circ, Back, Elastic and Bounce) and a mode (ease in, ease out, in and out, out and in). The Object tab draws the curve and plays it. **Go back after** plays the motion in reverse later, for doors that shut and lifts that come back down.
- **On arrive** acts when a group's motion ends, so motions can follow one another: a lift rises, then its door opens.
- Select a Move trigger to see where it sends its group, and drag the diamond to set the distance. The logic preview plays motions and outlines where each moved group belongs.
- The new **Motion** tab in the palette holds ready-made Door, Shuttle, Piston, Lift, Home, Drop, Spinner, Fader, Blink and Then pieces.
- Scripts can call `move_map_group`, `turn_map_group` and `fade_map_group`, and JSON hooks have a `map_motion` action.
- Moving cover adds almost no network traffic: snapshots carry each motion rather than every wall's position, and every client plays the same glide. Bots re-plan their routes while cover moves.

The [workshop guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.0.0/docs/map-editor.md#motions) explains motions, and the [modding guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.0.0/docs/modding.md#motions) has the map format.

## Lights and particles

- Lights brighten what lies under them: strongly in dark arenas and softly in light ones. **Flicker** makes a light waver like a flame or a failing lamp. The Decor tab has new Torch, Neon and Lantern presets.
- Particles can be soft or star-shaped, glow, drift sideways and twinkle. They can start anywhere in the emitter, inside its ellipse, along its outline or at its center. There are six new presets: Fireflies, Smoke, Snow, Glitter, Fountain and Pulse.
- Maps crowded with lights draw the extra ones more simply rather than slowing the frame, as maps crowded with particles already did.

## Workshop

- The workshop remembers your palette tab and item, the inspector tab, the Hidden and Animate switches, the logic preview's speed and where you were looking in the map, along with panel sizes.
- **Ctrl+Shift+A** selects everything like the selection, such as every torch or every speed pad.

## Tanks and bosses

- Automatic turrets sharing one mount are drawn as a single turret with a ringed cap, so their barrels no longer cross over each other. Fortress Array, Palisade, Tri-Sniper, Interceptor and Monolith look much cleaner.
- Boss armor has a raised rim. Wounded bosses glow amber, then pulse red in their last phase. Defender's turrets no longer cross over one another.

## Other changes

- On FreeBSD, the graphics settings explain that NVIDIA ships DLSS only for Linux and Windows.
- `tankyard-models --bosses --phase 3` draws bosses in a later phase.
- In workshop plugins, `editor.free_group` skips groups that motions and On arrive triggers use.
- Creator and central-owner identities are verified without asking only on the official server, servers on the same computer and games you host. Any other server asks first, so a server cannot pass your proof on to another server without your consent.

## Compatibility

Protocol **26**. Servers and players on 1.0 play only with each other, so players on 0.15 or 0.16 need to update before joining. Hosts should update their servers and relays together. Maps from earlier versions open unchanged, but maps saved or exported from 1.0 need 1.0.
