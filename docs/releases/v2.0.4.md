# Tank Yard v2.0.4

Smoother boss fire, configurable Boss Rush lineups, and saved maps for parties and offline games.

Boss fire bends along its flight path, Boss Rush lets you choose the lineup, and parties can play your saved maps and switch maps between rounds.

## Boss effects

- Trails follow the path each round has flown, smoothing ordinary turns while keeping sharp bounces against walls. Their ends taper and fade smoothly as a round slows or disappears.
- Heavy cannon rounds gain twisting light and flame that move with their trails.
- Soft glows have smoother color transitions on both light and dark floors.
- Tanks hide the light beneath them, including along long or offset barrels. The light mask reuses their drawn geometry, and skips work when there is nothing to hide.
- Corrected visibility and overlap checks at ribbon bends, polygon rims and the screen edge.

## Your Boss Rush

- **Classic six**, **Quick three**, or **Full rush** with all fourteen bosses.
- Choose individual bosses, change their order, adjust the count, or shuffle the lineup. Your choice is saved for later games.
- The round banner shows the current wave, boss and total, and announces who comes next.
- Long lineups reach the same final difficulty increase as the classic six. Shorter lineups keep the usual 14% increase per wave.
- A boss that has nowhere safe to spawn waits for room. If the space stays blocked, the round ends in a draw instead of getting stuck or skipping a boss.

Dedicated servers can set `boss_rush = ["prism", "siege_engine", "trapper"]` above the first TOML section, use `--boss-rush all`, or change the next round through the `boss-rush` console command. See [playing](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.4/docs/playing.md#boss-rush) and [hosting](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.4/docs/hosting.md).

## Party and map choices

- **Change map…** offers built-in arenas, saved Arena Workshop maps and map packs before creating a party or starting its next round.
- Party guests receive the selected map automatically, including when they join later. Existing invites and connections survive a map change.
- The leader's chosen Boss Rush lineup applies to the party.
- Map packs are prepared asynchronously so the client can keep handling menus and networking while it waits. Invalid settings or packs leave the current round intact.
- Saved maps chosen from the same picker also launch correctly in offline play.

See [parties](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.4/docs/parties.md).

## Compatibility and validation

This release keeps **protocol 34**, compatible with other 2.0 clients and servers. Custom Boss Rush lineups and party map changes require a host running 2.0.4; the visual improvements run in the client. Existing maps, packs and settings carry over.

Before release, 984 Rust tests passed with four existing hardware or environment tests ignored. Formatting, strict Clippy, installer tests, content checks, the optimized workspace build and Windows/macOS compilation checks passed. Linux visual checks covered light and dark themes, enlarged menus, live party map switching and all sixteen game modes. Windows and macOS runtime verification is performed by the release workflow's installation checks.
