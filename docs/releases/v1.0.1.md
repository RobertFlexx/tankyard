# Tank Yard v1.0.1

Bacteria slips into a private pocket dimension, the motions tutorial has a video, and doors and returning motions behave more reliably. Everything in [v1.0.0](v1.0.0.md) is included.

## Bacteria

- A new level-55 tank, reached from Specter or Wisp, with twin alternating cannons and a **ten-second pocket dimension**.
- The entry sound and brief blackout lead into a private, Backrooms-inspired maze with yellow wallpaper, worn carpet, fluorescent lights and eerie room tones. Ceiling fixtures pass above the tank, with light pools on the carpet and slight parallax. The textures and recorded light hum are CC0 assets.
- Repair 40% of your maximum hull health over the visit and reposition through the maze. You cannot fight, take damage or capture objectives inside. Carried cores drop and deployed weapons clear on entry.
- The cooldown is 40 seconds from entry, leaving thirty seconds outside between visits. A countdown and a small return ring announce your return.
- Sounds are local, follow volume settings and fade on return. Reduced motion keeps lights steady. [Bacteria guide](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.0.1/docs/bacteria.md).

## Workshop

- Doors and faders with **Go back after** return home even if another tank arrives while they are open or on their way back. A new arrival extends the wait without moving the resting point.
- The palette's **Door** moves its group to a fixed offset, so repeated arrivals keep it open at the same place.
- **On arrive** no longer acts when a motion has nowhere to go, such as a door told to open while it is already open. An interrupted motion still does not count as arriving.
- Particles' **Fade out** now controls their fade.
- Motion destinations and portal exits have diamond handles, matching the editor's guides.

## Tutorials

- The [motions tutorial](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.0.1/docs/motions-tutorial.md) builds a gatehouse with a sliding door, moving cover, chained motions, lights and particles.
- Its [video](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v1.0.1/docs/motions-tutorial.mp4) runs for 2 minutes 15 seconds, with English captions and seven chapters on the [website](https://robertflexx.github.io/tankyard-site/#motions-video). Choose **Motions** above the workshop's player.
- The website's [interactive motions guide](https://robertflexx.github.io/tankyard-site/#motions) demonstrates every curve and mode, all ten Motion pieces, chained motions, lights and particle presets.

## Compatibility

Protocol **27** adds the pocket timer and ability. Update clients, servers and any separate relays together; 1.0.0 and earlier cannot join a 1.0.1 server. Older peers receive an update notice. Existing 1.0 maps open unchanged.
