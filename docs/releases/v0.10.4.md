# Tank Yard v0.10.4

Native Metal rendering and MetalFX upscaling on Macs.

## Macs

- **Metal renderer, on by default.** Tank Yard used to draw with OpenGL, which macOS only emulates on top of Metal. It now draws with Metal directly. On an Apple M5 at 2560×1664 with 30 bots, a frame takes about 1.6 ms of work instead of 10 ms at the same settings, and the same picture. Less GPU work means cooler, quieter Macs and longer battery life.
- **MetalFX upscaling**: on macOS 13 or later with a supported GPU, the Upscaler setting offers Apple's MetalFX, and the Performance and Balanced presets use it. Elsewhere those presets use FSR 1 as before.
- MSAA, FXAA, FSR 1, sharpening, render scale and anisotropic texture filtering all work with Metal.
- **Settings → Graphics → Metal renderer** switches back to OpenGL after a restart, as does `--safe-graphics`. A Mac without a Metal GPU starts with OpenGL automatically.

Windows and Linux are unchanged and keep OpenGL.

Protocol **22**, unchanged: v0.10.x clients and servers play together.
