# Tank Yard v2.1.1

A small stability release. Everything in [v2.1.0](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.1/docs/releases/v2.1.0.md) is included.

## Fix

- Fixed a client crash on a boss's killing blow, including Railgunner. The boss health bar's damage trail could round its two ends a float step apart, which made the gradient clamp panic. The ends are now kept in order before drawing.
- Added a regression test that draws a square-ended damage trail at the rounding boundary where the crash happened.

## Compatibility

Multiplayer still uses **protocol 35**. Tank Yard 2.1.1 and 2.1.0 clients and servers can play together; hosts and players on 2.0 builds still need to update together.

See [hosting](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.1/docs/hosting.md), [bosses](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.1/docs/bosses-and-learning.md) and [modding](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.1.1/docs/modding.md).
