# Tank Yard v2.0.1

Everything that glows got a second look, the Siege Engine's shell is now properly on fire, and your party can finally hear you complain from the other side of the map. It plays with 2.0.0, so nobody has to update at the same moment.

## Bosses

- **Better fireworks.** Shots, muzzle flashes, hull lights, warnings and explosions on all fourteen bosses are redrawn with the game's glow lighting, and they stay bright on dark maps.
- **The Siege Engine's round is on fire.** It burns the whole way there, drags a flame trail behind it and leaves a scorch mark where you used to be.
- **Smoother movement.** Bosses ease through turns, sidestep your shots without twitching and steer around walls instead of grinding along them. Fights last about as long as they did.
- **Health bars you can read.** Notches mark where the next phase starts, and a pale stretch shows the damage you just did.
- **Arrows to bosses off screen,** with their names and health, so you can find the fight again after a respawn. Or avoid it. Nobody's judging.
- **Parting words.** Every boss now has something to say on your death screen. None of it is nice.

## Party voice

- Everyone in a party hears each other at full volume, anywhere: across the arena, while down, in a menu or between rounds.
- A mute button with the usual microphone icon is on the party rail, in the party panel and in the match, and **M** still works. Your mic starts muted, so nobody hears you eating.
- A ring around a member's tank shows who's talking. Muting someone only mutes them for you, and they never need to find out.
- The party's leader needs 2.0.1 for this. With a 2.0.0 leader, voice works by distance as before.

See [Voice](https://github.com/RobertFlexx/tankyard/blob/v2.0.1/docs/parties.md#voice).

## Arena Workshop

- **A Links view you can actually follow.** Busy channels gather at hubs, wires heading the same way run together as cables, and a key in the corner hides or shows each kind of link. Point at a hub to lift its wires out of the spaghetti.
- **Gates look like gates.** Every piece in the Gates and Circuits palettes has its own symbol.
- **Connections.** The inspector lists what a trigger sets off and what sets it off. Click a row to go there.
- **Clicks take the smallest thing under the pointer,** so a trigger sitting on a spawn region no longer needs a rescue mission.
- The layer bar opens a strip of every layer in use, and just selecting something no longer marks your map as edited.

## Graphics and speed

- **NVIDIA DLAA** has its own button in Graphics.
- A typical match now takes about half the draw calls and a twentieth of the memory allocations per frame. The picture is exactly the same, which was the point.

## Compatibility

Tank Yard 2.0.1 still uses **protocol 34**, so it plays with 2.0.0 clients and servers, and maps and packs carry over unchanged.
