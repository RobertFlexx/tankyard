Ready to roll

Open Tank Yard from Applications or Launchpad. Choose Play for the public server, or Play offline for a local game.

Host a server

In the game, open Host server, then Community server. To run one from Terminal instead:
"/Applications/Tank Yard.app/Contents/MacOS/tankyard-server"

Uninstall

Move Tank Yard from Applications to the Trash. Your settings stay in your Library folder.
